Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How can I track my order?

Visit Your Account → Orders to view your order history and current order status.

How do I update my payment method?

Go to Your Account → Payment Methods to add, edit, or remove saved payment methods.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders may be canceled before they have shipped. Visit your order history or contact customer support.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically arrives in 3–5 business days. Express shipping arrives in 1–2 business days.

How do I change my password?

Visit Your Account → Profile & Login Information and select Change Password.

How do I return a product?

Contact our support team with your order number to begin the return process.
